Embedded model atlases reprojected through source geometry/UVs to equirectangular
maps, with 2K/1K optimized WebP exports. NASA media usage guidelines:
https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/
NASA does not endorse ASTRAEA. No NASA logos or insignia are used.
Cached source GLBs in docs/source-maps are not deployed.
Existing five-destination hero maps retain credits in planet-credits.txt.

RESORT LIGHTING: 16 fictional photographic-style architectural variants created
with built-in image generation from the project's existing resort compositions.
The 16 exact prompts and original PNG paths are in docs/lighting-assets.json.
Original generated files are retained; exports use 1536px / 768px WebP.
Four original base states complete the 20-state set. The asset optimizer is
docs/prepare_lighting_assets.py. These images are art-directed concepts, not
real resorts, scientific observations, or measured physical lighting results.
Architecture and celestial scale remain illustrative. AI image variations can
introduce small geometric differences between states.

The launch spacecraft and terminal are procedural WebGL geometry authored in
scene-renderer.js and departure-scene.js; no imported high-detail model is claimed.
