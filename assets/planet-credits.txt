ASTRAEA PLANETARY ASSETS / SOURCE & MODIFICATION RECORD

LUNA: NASA Scientific Visualization Studio, CGI Moon Kit (2019).
LROC WAC color mosaic and LOLA elevation data; visualization: Ernie Wright (USRA).
https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720/
Sources: lroc_color_poles_4k.tif and ldem_16_uint.tif.
Local modifications: bounded multiscale local contrast, a subtle cold grade,
resizing/WebP, tangent normals from LOLA elevation (2x relief), and roughness
approximation. Lunar imagery is not synthetic cratering.

EUROPA: NASA/JPL-Caltech, NASA Science Europa 3D Model, Visualization Technology
Applications and Development (VTAD). Embedded color_2016_03_10 map.
https://science.nasa.gov/resource/europa-3d-model/
Local modifications: neutral/cool grade, bounded multiscale contrast to reveal
existing fractures, resizing/WebP, locally normalized high-pass micro-normals,
and separate clean-ice/mineral-fracture roughness. These micro-normals are
artistic approximations, NOT measured Europa elevations. No new fracture
networks are drawn; the organic fractures are in the source map.

TITAN: NASA/JPL-Caltech, NASA Science Titan 3D Model, VTAD.
https://science.nasa.gov/resource/titan-3d-model/
Local modifications: resizing/WebP and amplification of resolved broad cloud
and latitude contrast from the source. A restrained, seamless procedural
aerosol field and polar haze hood add broad optical-depth variation. These
are artistic atmosphere enhancements, not new observed features or terrain.
Layered atmospheric scattering is rendered in real time; it is an artistic
physical approximation, not a climate model.

SATURN COLOR AND RINGS: NASA/JPL-Caltech, NASA Science Saturn 3D Model, VTAD.
https://science.nasa.gov/resource/saturn-3d-model/
The embedded saturn_diff.jpg cube atlas is reprojected using the original
model's mesh and UV coordinates, accounting for the source oblate shape.
Native cloud bands, small storms, and polar detail receive bounded local
contrast enhancement and are exported at 1024/2048/4096 pixel widths.
Embedded saturn_rings_25sat_outterRings strip. Modified: native 4096-sample radial
strip, source-derived radial detail contrast, unsaturated optical density,
soft edges and a low-density Cassini Division at approximately 1.95-2.03 radii.

MARS COLOR: Solar System Scope / INOVE, NASA-derived planetary map.
https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/
Licensed CC BY 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
Mars: 8k_mars.jpg (download mirror used because origin returned HTTP 403):
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Shriisoot/Planets-texture/main/8k_mars.jpg
Local modifications: Mars saturation reduced; bounded multiscale local contrast
reveals existing terrain. The previous Solar System Scope Saturn color map was
replaced by the NASA model atlas above; its source remains cached for provenance.
Resized to 1024/2048/4096 texture tiers; Mars micro-normal/roughness derived only
from locally normalized high-pass image detail, NOT a measured MOLA elevation
map. Broad albedo regions are removed before deriving normal slopes, which are
limited to avoid implausible high ridges around dark mineral boundaries.

NASA source imagery is used under NASA's images/media usage guidelines:
https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/
NASA/JPL do not endorse ASTRAEA. No NASA logos or insignia are included.
All color grading, lighting, scales and atmosphere parameters are artistic.
Enhancements improve the visibility of source detail; they cannot recover
features absent from the original imagery. In particular, Europa's source
coverage varies in resolution and Titan's visible atmosphere is naturally soft.
Original source caches are excluded from the production build. The local
optimized derivatives and this attribution file must be distributed together.
